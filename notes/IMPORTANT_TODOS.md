- [x] Game loss doesnt work at all. When one player dies their board state gets stuck on the opponent's client. On their client, they keep seeing the scoreboard pop up over and over again but the round doesnt progress. When a player dies scoreboard is stuck with 0 points for both players. — FIXED. Two interrelated bugs: (1) a top-out finalized the shared `GameRunner` (singleplayer end-of-run semantics set `ended` permanently), so after the next round's `game_start` restored a fresh board the runner refused to tick — the dead player became a frozen zombie and the round could never resolve. `GameRunner.reset()` now un-finalizes it on a round change (`MultiplayerGameScreen`). (2) The intermission dismissal was one-sided: the UI cleared its local copy after ~4s but `MatchClient.state.intermission` stayed set, so any later relay (e.g. the opponent's ~10Hz board_update) resurrected the scoreboard. `client.clearIntermission()` now keeps the store in sync. Covered by a `GameRunner` unit test and a `MatchClient` test; suite 261 green.
- [x] When you refresh or rejoin the page, there is no popup to rejoin your last lobby. — FIXED. Root cause: when a page refresh's new socket reached the server BEFORE the old socket's `close`, the player wasn't in the rejoin `buffered` map yet, so `hello` treated them as brand-new and never sent `rejoin_offer`. `server/index.ts` now also offers a rejoin when the presented `selfId` is a currently-live member (close still in flight), materializing the buffer on the spot so the popup (+ REJOIN/DISMISS + grace) all work. E2E proven: a reconnect that beats its own close still surfaces the popup.
- [x] When a player manually rejoins a lobby after going into the reconnecting state, there are two copies of that player, where one is reconnecting and the other is the new player state. — FIXED. Same root cause as above: the refresh racing its own close fell through to a brand-new id (and the delayed old close buffered the `reconnecting` copy), so a fallback `join_lobby` added a second member. With `hello` now claiming the rejoinId of a live member, the identity is preserved (no fresh id, no re-join as a new player), and the delayed old socket's close is treated as superseded (`conns.get(conn.id) !== conn`) so it can neither re-buffer nor re-mark `reconnecting`, nor yank the live claimer's routing. E2E proven: exactly one member, reconnect flag cleared, no duplicate before or after the delayed close.
